Who this is for

Small defense contractors and subs (1–50 people) who handle FCI: purchase orders, drawings, schedules, contract data. Primes are starting to ask subs for their Level 1 status before they will issue a subcontract.

What Level 1 actually requires

Fifteen basic safeguards from FAR 52.204-21: access control, MFA, visitor logs, firewalls, patching, antivirus, and media disposal. All 15 must be MET, because Level 1 allows no POA&Ms. Then you post the result in SPRS and affirm it every year.
