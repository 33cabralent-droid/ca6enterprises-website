What we supply

Supplies, equipment, and the support that goes with them, for a Department of War or Department of Defense requirement.

Supplies

Equipment

Gear and hardware the requirement names.

Services

Related support that goes with the item, when the requirement asks for it.

What to send

A short note is enough. These three details are what we need to tell you if we can fill it.

  1. The item. A description, NSN, or the solicitation itself.
  2. The quantity and place. How many, and where they have to go.
  3. The date. When it is needed.
