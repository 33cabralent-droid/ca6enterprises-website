Who We Are

Built for
the Mission.

CA6 ENTERPRISES LLC is a Florida limited liability company based in Melbourne, on Florida's Space Coast. Our focus is simple: get defense buyers the logistics items they need.

We work from the requirement in front of you. Send the item, part number, NSN, or solicitation, the quantity and delivery point, and the date you need it, and we'll respond with what we can supply.
